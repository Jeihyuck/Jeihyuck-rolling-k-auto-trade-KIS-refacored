failures. All these responses explicitly record `broker_submit=false`.
PREP checkpoint `kr_prep_core_ok:practice:2026-10-16:2026-10-15` has 30 rows,
contract/quality/DB roundtrip/file mirror checks all successful. PREP absence
therefore does not explain Oct 16's submission failure.

Sample Oct 16 failed runs create their first order intent about 154–169 seconds
after `runs.started_at`; examples finish at 199–209 seconds. These timestamps
exclude some runner setup and do not represent signal-to-POST latency.
The `fills` table has no rows with effective timestamp
`coalesce(filled_at, created_at::timestamptz)` in the queried KST week.
This does not establish absence of broker executions: the price-unresolved
orders are evidence of an incomplete reconciliation chain, not permission to
invent prices or PnL. The DB also records 50 Oct 16 reconciliations degraded by
`unresolved_execution_actions`.

- A: incorporate original pre-submit classification, shared deadline and removal
  of full-holdings blocking prewarm onto the current baseline. Queue the initial
  diagnostic exit snapshot until finalization, after order work. Recheck tail
  budget before each diagnostic write and discard skipped diagnostics at tick end.
  Risk-state writes, intent, claim, broker observations and settlement remain
  synchronous. No old uncertain claim is automatically released.
- A CI: run KR contract CI for both stacked base branches and explicitly include
  the Oct 13/18 offline execution regression files.
- B: preserve current US collector readiness changes during integration. Wait for
  explicit unsubscribe success before freeing a KR slot; reconnect on rejection,
  missing/invalid success or ACK timeout. Pace unsubscribe requests, preserve
  already active non-KR slots and never exceed socket capacity. Bound REST quote
  rate/jitter sleeps and inflight joins by the existing shared deadline.
- C: retarget to B; apply start-aligned waiting to direct/wrapped timeout recovery
  and recoverable exception paths as well as successful and no-trade paths.

proof that the next trading session will execute correctly.

## Required reconciliation invariants

1. Preserve the 5 uncertain actions and their 184 requested shares. A new typed

2. Preserve exact broker IDs and confirmed quantities of the 10 unpriced SELLs
   recorded across Oct 13–14. Attribute execution prices only from exact broker
   fills; never use quotes, average cost or balance deltas as synthetic prices.
3. Apply late genuine fills even when their original tick/generation is old,
   deduplicating by broker execution identity and account/order/date scope.
4. A timeout, restart, age threshold or absent first query page never releases
   ambiguous submission fences. No cleanup migration or DB rewrite is included.
